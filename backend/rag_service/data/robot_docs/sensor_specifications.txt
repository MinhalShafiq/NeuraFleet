NeuraFleet Sensor Specifications
==================================

LiDAR Sensor: Velodyne VLP-16 (Puck)
--------------------------------------
- Type: 16-channel 3D spinning LiDAR
- Range: 1 m to 100 m
- Accuracy: +/- 3 cm (typical)
- Horizontal field of view: 360 degrees
- Vertical field of view: 30 degrees (-15 to +15 degrees)
- Angular resolution (horizontal): 0.1 to 0.4 degrees (configurable)
- Angular resolution (vertical): 2 degrees between channels
- Scan rate: 5 Hz to 20 Hz (default 10 Hz)
- Points per second: up to 300,000
- Wavelength: 903 nm (near-infrared, Class 1 eye-safe)
- Weight: 830 g
- Operating temperature: -10 C to 60 C
- Power consumption: 8 W typical
- Data output: UDP packets over Ethernet (100 Mbps)

IMU Sensor: Bosch BNO055 9-axis Absolute Orientation
------------------------------------------------------
- Accelerometer: 3-axis, range +/- 16 g, resolution 1 mg, noise 150 ug/sqrt(Hz)
- Gyroscope: 3-axis, range +/- 2000 deg/s, resolution 1/16 deg/s
- Magnetometer: 3-axis, range +/- 1300 uT (x/y), +/- 2500 uT (z)
- Sample rate: up to 1000 Hz (accelerometer), 523 Hz (gyroscope)
- Onboard sensor fusion: Kalman-filter-based AHRS
- Interface: I2C / UART
- Operating temperature: -40 C to 85 C
- Power consumption: 12.3 mA at 3.3 V

GPS Receiver: u-blox ZED-F9P (RTK-capable)
--------------------------------------------
- Horizontal accuracy: 2.0 m CEP (standalone), 0.01 m + 1 ppm (RTK fixed)
- Vertical accuracy: 3.5 m CEP (standalone), 0.015 m + 1 ppm (RTK fixed)
- Maximum update rate: 25 Hz
- Supported constellations: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou
- Cold start time-to-first-fix: 24 seconds (typical)
- Sensitivity: -167 dBm (tracking)
- Interface: UART / USB / SPI
- Operating temperature: -40 C to 85 C

Temperature Sensors
-------------------
- Type: NTC thermistor array (4 sensors per robot)
- Locations: motor housing, battery pack, main PCB, external ambient
- Range: -40 C to 125 C
- Accuracy: +/- 0.5 C (at 25 C)
- Resolution: 0.1 C
- Sample rate: 10 Hz
- Alert thresholds: Warning at 75 C, Critical at 85 C, Shutdown at 95 C
